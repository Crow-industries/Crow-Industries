THE PARENT COMPANY

About CROW Industries

CROW Industries serves as the parent identity for three independent divisions. Each division can develop its own projects and visual identity while remaining part of the larger organization.

Independent divisions

Gaming, Technologies, and Engineering each have separate areas of focus.

Shared foundation

The divisions share the CROW Industries parent organization, portal, and broader company direction.

Development approach

Projects are introduced as they become ready. Incomplete sections are labeled clearly.

Visual identity

The company uses a dark industrial foundation. Each division has its own accent color: gold, cyan, and violet.

SYSTEMS & AUTOMATION

Logic Guide Book

Logic blocks process signals from sensors and other logic blocks. The examples here focus on harmless indicators, displays, and general vehicle automation.

How signals work

The wiki describes logic outputs as signal values. For ordinary gates, the combined inputs determine whether the gate produces an output. Math blocks can process numeric values beyond the ordinary gate signal range. Check each block's in-game configuration for its available timing, delay, input, and output settings.
